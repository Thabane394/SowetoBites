About SowetoBites

Our Story: SowetoBites was founded to bring authentic township flavors to the heart of Soweto. We started with one grill and a dream to serve the best kotas in the hood.

Our Mission

To serve affordable, delicious, and fast street food while creating jobs and supporting our local community.

Our Vision

To become Soweto's most trusted takeaway brand known for quality, speed, and good vibes.
