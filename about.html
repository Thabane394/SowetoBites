About SowetoBites

Our Story

Our Story: SowetoBites was founded in 2019 to bring authentic township flavors to the heart of Soweto. We started with one grill and a dream to serve the best kotas in the hood. Our founder grew up in Orlando West, learning family recipes from his grandmother who sold magwinya on Vilakazi Street in the 1980s.

What began as a small container shop now serves over 200 customers daily. We still use the same wood-fire grill and secret spice mix passed down for three generations. Every Kota is made with love and Soweto pride.

Our Mission

To serve affordable, delicious, and fast street food while creating jobs and supporting our local community. We source all our bread, meat, and vegetables from Soweto local suppliers, creating a circular township economy.

Our Vision

To become Soweto's most trusted takeaway brand known for quality, speed, and good vibes. In 5 years, we want to franchise to 10 townships while keeping our Vilakazi Street roots.

Our Values

Ubuntu: Community first. Quality: Fresh daily. Affordability: Student prices. Culture: Preserving Kasi flavours.

Why Vilakazi Street?

Vilakazi Street is the only street in the world where two Nobel Peace Prize winners lived - Nelson Mandela and Archbishop Desmond Tutu. Located in Orlando West, Soweto, it is a hub of South African history and culture. SowetoBites is proud to be part of this legacy, serving tourists and locals who visit the Hector Pieterson Museum and Mandela House.

What is Kota? Kota, also known as Bunny Chow, is a quarter loaf of bread hollowed out and filled with chips, cheese, polony, Russian sausage, and atchar. It originated in Durban but became iconic Soweto street food in the 1970s. Our Kota Special (R45) is our tribute to this history.

Meet the Team

We are 5 young people from Soweto - cooks, drivers, and dreamers. When you buy from SowetoBites, you support local youth employment.
