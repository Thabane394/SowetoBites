About SowetoBites

Our Story

Our Story: SowetoBites was founded in 2019 to bring authentic township flavors to the heart of Soweto. We started with one grill and a dream to serve the best kotas in the hood. Our founder grew up in Orlando West, learning family recipes from his grandmother who sold magwinya on Vilakazi Street in the 1980s.

What began as a small container shop now serves over 200 customers daily. We still use the same wood-fire grill and secret spice mix passed down for three generations.

Our Mission

To serve affordable, delicious, and fast street food while creating jobs and supporting our local community.

Our Vision

To become Soweto's most trusted takeaway brand known for quality, speed, and good vibes.

Our Values

Ubuntu: Community first. Quality: Fresh daily. Affordability: Student prices.

Why Vilakazi Street?

Vilakazi Street is the only street in the world where two Nobel Peace Prize winners lived - Nelson Mandela and Archbishop Desmond Tutu. SowetoBites is proud to be part of this legacy.

What is Kota? Kota is a quarter loaf of bread hollowed out and filled with chips, cheese, polony, Russian and atchar. It became iconic Soweto street food in the 1970s.
